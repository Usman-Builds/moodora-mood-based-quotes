Moodora finds the right words for how you feel right now. Choose from eight moods (Happy, Calm, Hopeful, Grateful, Sad, Anxious, Angry and Lonely) and discover handpicked quotes that speak to that moment, with 1,600 quotes from writers, thinkers and voices across history. Whether you are celebrating, searching for calm, or getting through a hard day, Moodora shows you one quote at a time in a soft dark design that is easy on your eyes, morning or night. Every quote is built into the app, so it works offline with no account or sign up, and it never asks for your name, email, location or contacts. Find words that resonate and let your mood lead the way.
